Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Overview

Moment Bloom does not require an account and does not collect, upload, sell, or share personal data. Countdown settings and custom firework text are processed on your device.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your language preference and a local record of whether Lifetime Pro has been unlocked. This information remains on your device and is not sent to the developer.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. The app receives the purchase status needed to unlock Lifetime Pro. The developer does not receive or store your payment card details.

Permissions, analytics, and advertising

Moment Bloom does not request access to your camera, photos, microphone, contacts, or location. It does not include advertising, analytics, or cross-app tracking.

Children’s privacy

Because the app does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

This website

This static website uses no analytics, advertising, or cookies set by Moment Bloom. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process technical request information under GitHub’s own privacy terms.

If you contact support through GitHub Issues, the information you post is public and processed by GitHub. Do not include receipts, order numbers, email addresses, or other personal information.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app’s data practices change. For questions, visit the support page.

你的时刻，只属于你

隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 9 月 22 日

概述

时刻烟花无需注册账号，不收集、上传、出售或共享个人数据。倒计时设置和自定义烟花文字均在你的设备上处理。

保存在设备上的信息

App 会保存你的语言偏好，以及 Lifetime Pro 是否已解锁的本地记录。这些信息仅保留在设备上，不会发送给开发者。

购买

App 内购买由 Apple App Store 或 Google Play 处理。App 仅接收用于解锁 Lifetime Pro 的购买状态；开发者不会收到或保存你的支付卡信息。

权限、分析与广告

时刻烟花不请求访问相机、相册、麦克风、通讯录或位置，也不包含广告、分析或跨 App 跟踪。

儿童隐私

由于 App 不收集个人数据，因此也不会有意收集儿童的个人数据。

本网站

本静态网站不使用分析、广告，也不由时刻烟花设置 Cookie。网站由 GitHub Pages 托管，GitHub 可能按其隐私条款处理技术请求信息。

如果你通过 GitHub Issues 联系支持，发布的内容将公开并由 GitHub 处理。请勿提交收据、订单号、邮箱或其他个人信息。

变更与联系

当 App 的数据处理方式发生变化时，本政策可能更新。如有问题，请访问支持页面。
