Moment Bloom · Countdown & fireworks

How do I start a countdown?

Choose Duration countdown, Custom time range, or Target time. Set the time and firework style, then tap Start countdown.

How do I leave a countdown or fireworks screen?

Use the close button in the top-left corner. You can also long-press the countdown screen or tap the fireworks screen.

What does Lifetime Pro unlock?

Lifetime Pro is a one-time, non-consumable purchase that unlocks custom multiline text fireworks and all text-firework styles. It is not a subscription.

How do I restore Lifetime Pro?

Open Settings, then tap Restore purchases. Make sure the device is signed in to the same Apple Account or Google Account used for the original purchase.

A purchase is still pending

Some purchases require approval or additional processing by the store. The app unlocks Lifetime Pro after the store reports the purchase as completed.

时刻烟花 · 倒计时与烟花

如何开始倒计时？

选择“时长倒计时”“指定时间段”或“指定结束时间”，设置时间和烟花效果，然后点击“开始倒计时”。

如何退出倒计时或烟花页面？

点击左上角的关闭按钮。你也可以长按倒计时页面，或点击烟花页面退出。

Lifetime Pro 解锁什么？

Lifetime Pro 是一次性非消耗型购买，用于解锁自定义多行文字烟花和全部文字烟花样式，不是订阅。

如何恢复 Lifetime Pro？

打开“设置”，点击“恢复购买”。请确保设备已登录原购买时使用的 Apple 账号或 Google 账号。

购买仍处于等待状态

部分购买需要家长批准或商店进一步处理。商店确认交易完成后，App 会自动解锁 Lifetime Pro。
